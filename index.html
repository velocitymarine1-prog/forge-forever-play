The Forge

Forge Forever, pass 6. Any two things forge on the anvil: a weapon and an ingredient, two weapons (the left one is the base and takes one gift), two ingredients. From level 25 the Crucible melts two rare weapons of different classes and a Legend Ember into a legend, and the first smith in the world to pour one may name it.

Alone from disk this page is a world of one: pairs the seed ledger knows reveal the Forge Oracle's results, and everything else is the offline Combiner's provisional draft. Connect to the local world service to forge against the shared world.
